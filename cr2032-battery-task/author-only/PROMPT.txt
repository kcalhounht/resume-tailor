Returned CR2032 sensor nodes die far earlier than the architecture review claimed. The 25°C bench capture, power architecture, production BOM, part currents, and CR2032 rules are in /app/data.

Find the condition that keeps the sleep drain high, and give the battery life in days that the corrected build holds at 25°C.

Write /app/answer.json with exactly these fields:

{
  "condition": "<one sentence>",
  "as_built_sleep_uA": <number>,
  "fixed_sleep_uA": <number>,
  "life_days": <number>
}

as_built_sleep_uA is the sleep-plateau cell current of the captured unit.
fixed_sleep_uA is the deep-sleep cell current after that condition is corrected.
life_days uses battery.md on the corrected build. The wake burst does not change.
